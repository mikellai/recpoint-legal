RecPoint

Mark this moment. Find your way back.

Just parked, found a great spot, or want to remember an entrance? One tap saves your current location, and you can add photos and notes. Later, a compass shows you the direction and distance, or you can open a maps app with one tap to navigate back. All of your data stays on your iPhone.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the arrow on the Find page mean?

The arrow points toward your destination, and the distance shown is the straight-line distance, not a walking or driving route, so follow the actual roads to get there. If you need a route, tap the navigation button to open Apple Maps or Google Maps.

Can I use it without an internet connection?

Saved names, times, notes and photos are stored on your device, so you can still view them offline. Maps and external navigation need an internet connection.

What happens to my data when I get a new phone?

In Settings, create a Full Backup and save it to the Files app or iCloud Drive, then restore it on your new phone. You can also back up your whole phone with iCloud Backup.
