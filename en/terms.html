3. Accuracy of location and direction

  • Location results depend on your device’s GPS, Wi-Fi, cellular and other signals. Indoors, in underground parking garages, among tall buildings or in mountainous areas, the error can be tens of meters or even more.
  • The Find page shows straight-line distance and direction, not a walking or driving route. The actual path may be blocked by buildings, rivers, roads or private property.
  • Directions rely on your device’s electronic compass, which can be affected by magnets, metal objects, phone cases or electronic equipment.
  • The App isn’t suitable for emergency rescue, hiking or mountaineering safety, marine navigation, aviation, or any other situation where precise positioning is needed to keep people safe.
