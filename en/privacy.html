Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

At a glance

  • RecPoint has no accounts and no servers. Your location records, names, notes and photos stay only on your device. We can’t see them and we can’t get them.
  • The app only uses your location while you’re using it. It doesn’t track your location in the background.
  • Ads in the app are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob processes some device and usage data under its own policies, but your location records, notes and photos are never provided to advertisers.
  • Whether to allow ad tracking is up to you. If you decline, you can still use every feature as normal.
  • When you buy Remove Ads, Apple handles the payment. We never receive your payment information.

1. Scope

This policy explains how the developer (“we”) of RecPoint (the “App”) handles the information generated when you use the App. By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

2. Data stored on your device

The following data is created by you in the App and stored in the App’s own private storage on your device. It is not sent to us:

DataWhat it includes
Location recordsLatitude and longitude, location accuracy, time created and modified, time zone
Record detailsThe name, type and notes you enter
PhotosCopies of photos you take or choose from your photo library. EXIF metadata (including the photo’s own GPS location and the device it was taken with) is removed when the photo is saved
PreferencesAppearance, distance units, preferred navigation app
In-app backupsFull Backup files you create manually (the 5 most recent are kept)

We don’t offer account sign-up, and we don’t run any servers that receive the data above. The App has no built-in analytics tools, and the debug messages the App logs never include coordinates, names, notes or file paths.

3. Permissions the app needs

You can turn off any permission at any time in your iPhone’s Settings. When a permission is off, the features that rely on it won’t work, but everything else is unaffected.

  • Location (While Using the App): Used only when you add a record, tap My Location, sort by distance, or when the Find page calculates direction and distance. The App never asks for “Always” access and doesn’t keep tracking your location in the background.
  • Camera: Used to take photos of things like your parking space, a street sign or an entrance and attach them to a record.
  • Photos: Used to choose photos from your library to add to a record. A copy of each photo is saved inside the App. We don’t read anything else in your photo library.

4. When data leaves your device

The App itself never uploads your data on its own. Data leaves the App only in the following situations, each triggered by something you do or by your system settings:

System backups

If you turn on iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, the App’s data (including records and photos) is backed up along with it through Apple’s standard process. These backups are handled by Apple and are subject to the Apple Privacy Policy.

Export and Full Backup

You can export your records as a CSV or GeoJSON file, or create a Full Backup file that includes photos, and save it to a location you choose. These files are not encrypted, so keep them safe and don’t send them to anyone you don’t trust.

Sharing

When you use the share feature, only the details you select (such as coordinates, time and notes) are included, and they’re sent through the app you choose.

External navigation

When you tap navigate, the App opens Apple Maps or Google Maps and sends only the destination’s latitude and longitude, without the name or notes. Route planning from there is handled by that map service and is subject to its privacy policy.

Map view

Maps in the App are displayed with Apple MapKit. To load map tiles, your device requests map data for the area currently on screen from Apple, and Apple handles this under its privacy policy.

Advertising

See the next section.

5. Advertising

To keep the App free, ads provided by Google AdMob (a service of Google LLC) are shown below the Records list. To serve ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may collect and process the following information:

  • Device information, such as device model, operating system version and language settings
  • IP address, and your approximate region estimated from your IP address
  • Ad interactions, such as whether an ad was shown and whether it was tapped
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA): collected only if you allow the App to track
  • Diagnostic data related to how the ad SDK runs, such as crash logs and performance data

We never provide advertisers with anything you create in the App, including location records, names, notes and photos. The precise locations you record are never used to serve ads either.

Your choices

  • Regional consent: If you’re in a region where consent is legally required first (such as the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom), the App asks for your consent through Google’s consent management tool before showing ads. You can change your choice later in Settings › Ad Privacy Settings.
  • App Tracking Transparency: Before the App shows an ad for the first time, iOS asks whether you allow tracking. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, AdMob won’t receive your advertising identifier and will only show non-personalized or contextual ads. You can change this later in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Personalized ads: You can manage Apple’s advertising settings in Settings › Privacy & Security › Apple Advertising.

To learn how Google uses this information, see the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

6. In-app purchases

You can pay for Remove Ads, a one-time purchase that lasts forever. Payments, receipts and refunds are all handled by Apple through your Apple Account, and we never receive your name, payment method or credit card details.

After your purchase, the App only remembers on your device that ads have been removed. Once you purchase or restore your purchase, the App stops showing ads and no longer asks for tracking permission for ads. If you switch phones or reinstall the App, you can get your purchase back from Apple in Settings › Restore Purchases.

7. Children’s privacy

The App isn’t designed specifically for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly target ads to children or collect information from them. If you’re a minor, please use the App with the consent and supervision of a parent or guardian.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Records you delete in the App are first moved to Recently Deleted and are permanently deleted automatically after 30 days. You can also clear them manually sooner.
  • In Settings › Erase All Data, you can delete all of your records and photos at once.
  • When you delete the App, all data stored in the App (including in-app backups) is deleted along with it. Files you exported or saved separately, and iCloud device backups, aren’t affected, and it’s up to you to manage them.

Because we don’t keep your data, once it’s deleted without a backup, we can’t recover it for you.

9. Data security

Your data is protected by the iOS app sandbox and device encryption. We recommend setting a passcode on your device. Please note that export files and Full Backup files are not encrypted and contain locations and photos, so store them somewhere safe.

10. Your rights

The data you create in the App is completely under your control. You can view, edit, export or delete it in the App at any time. Because we don’t keep this data, we can’t look it up or delete it on your behalf.

For ad-related data processed by Google AdMob, you can exercise your rights with Google in the ways described in the Google Privacy Policy. Your rights under the Personal Data Protection Act of Taiwan or other applicable laws are not affected by this policy. If you have any questions, feel free to contact us.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy because of feature changes or changes in the law. When we do, we’ll update the effective date at the top of this page. If a change significantly affects how data is handled, we’ll also let you know in the App.
